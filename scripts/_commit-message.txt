docs(suite): fold the suite-pattern into a section of technical.md

Mirror the cross-repo suite pattern (originally a standalone
doc/en/suite-pattern.md in teclatlon, folded into teclatlon's
technical.md section 8 in the previous commit) into the equivalent
technical docs of every other app of the Miralante suite.

The block is appended as the new last top-level section of each
file. Its number is computed from the highest existing `## N.`
in the destination doc (calculia section 8, memofun section 10,
okeymoney section 12, routime section 14, sinonimia ES section 12;
sinonimia EN keeps its unnumbered heading style). Sub-headings
(`### N.M` and `#### N.M.K`) are renumbered to match, so they don't
collide with any pre-existing `### N.M` in the destination doc.
The HTML `<title>Teclatlon - ...</title>` example inside the
block is rewritten to each app's name; the rest of the block
is verbatim.

Apptonomia is skipped: it does not ship `doc/en/technical.md`,
and the suite-pattern content is already covered by its own
`CLAUDE.md`.
